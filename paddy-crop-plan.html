Why N is planned ~10 kg above the need
Only 70–80% of organic N becomes available to the crop in the season, so 59–64 kg supplied lands close to the 50 kg the crop uses. For 60 : 20 : 20 areas (Cauvery delta, Coimbatore tract), raise basal vermicompost from 500 kg to 1 t/acre.
Slurry limits — do not exceed
Maximum 4,000 L per stage when green manure is used. Too much N at once gives soft, lodging-prone growth that attracts plant hoppers and blast. Release slurry slowly into the irrigation water while the field floods; never dump it at one spot near seedlings. If your slurry is thin (more water than 1:1 or rain-diluted), raise volumes by about 25%.
Minimum neem cake and ash — keep even with plenty of slurry
Neem cake 50 kg/acre at basal suppresses stem borer and nematodes, which slurry does not. Wood ash 25 kg at panicle initiation + 2 kg booting spray supplies silica for strong stems — slurry has none, and the extra N raises lodging risk. Draining the field 5–7 days at maximum tillering also toughens stems.
How to read this table
Only bulk inputs (green manure, slurry, vermicompost, neem cake, ash) are counted for NPK. Jeevamirtham, Panchagavyam and the amino sprays feed soil microbes and improve uptake, so they are listed but not added to the totals. One adult cow gives ~50 L slurry/day — 14,000 L per acre per season needs about 2–2.5 cows per acre.
